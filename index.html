Imad is an independent financial-markets and blockchain analyst, four years into reading order books, on-chain data, and price structure for a living. The foundation underneath it is a biochemical-analysis degree — the same close-reading, keep-the-records-straight discipline, just pointed at charts instead of samples.

The work combines fundamental and classical technical analysis, Wyckoff methodology, and Gann theory to read cryptocurrency and financial markets, with a personal portfolio managed under structured risk rules and AI tools folded into the research workflow. Fourteen certifications from Binance Academy and Coursera back the self-study — investment risk, portfolio management, blockchain, and the wider Web3 stack.

He publishes market analysis on Binance Square under the_unique_dz and runs a Web3-focused community on Telegram at @D1337.
